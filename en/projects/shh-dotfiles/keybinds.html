Keybinds

Super is the Windows key. Super+/ shows every key, with search; a new key in the configs shows up there by itself (keys). The keys work on any keyboard layout.

Files: config/sway/keybinds, config/sway/workspaces, the keys of each part in config/sway/features/; installed to ~/.config/sway/.

Keys of the parts

Turn a part off in shh config, e.g. record, and its keys go too: the next shh update removes its file ~/.config/sway/features/record, where they live. Until Super+Shift+C (or a relogin) sway still knows the old keys.
